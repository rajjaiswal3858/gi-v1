HatchWorks AI Wins at Global Generative AI Awards 2024

  • Andy Smith
  • October 2, 2024
  • Updated: August 4, 2025
Recognized for Pioneering Innovation with Generative-Driven Development™

Atlanta, GA, October 2, 2024 –- HatchWorks AI is honored to announce its win in the “Generative AI Solution Providers” category at the prestigious Global Generative AI Awards 2024.

This accolade underscores HatchWorks AI’s commitment to advancing innovation productivity through its innovative Generative-Driven Development™ methodology, optimizing business outcomes while reducing costs.

The Global Generative AI Awards highlighted groundbreaking contributions from around the world, showcasing how Generative AI is reshaping industries from healthcare to environmental sustainability.

HatchWorks AI stood out for its practical application of emerging technologies, bridging the gap between classical AI principles and modern software development.

Traditional software development can be time-consuming and often struggles to keep pace with rapidly changing business needs. Our Generative-Driven Development™ is all about accelerating this process without sacrificing quality. We leverage generative AI to automate repetitive tasks, generate code snippets, and provide real-time insights, enabling our developers to focus on solving the real challenges."

Omar Shanti, CTO of HatchWorks AI

HatchWorks AI’s Generative-Driven Development™ emphasizes:

  • Integration of Generative AI into Development Tools: By embedding generative AI directly into the development environment, HatchWorks AI enhances productivity and code quality.
  • Avoiding Over-Optimization: The methodology focuses on delivering functional solutions quickly, with the flexibility to iterate and improve based on real-world feedback.
  • Human-Centered Design: The approach ensures that technology serves the user first, aligning AI tools with actual user needs rather than forcing users to adapt to new technologies.


“It’s all about balancing innovation with usability,” added Shanti. “We believe in future-proof designs that accommodate emerging trends while recognizing the evolving nature of AI and software. Think of this as paving a path that adapts as new technologies unfold. The real value lies in delivering solutions that are not just cutting-edge but also immediately useful and scalable.”

About Global Generative AI Awards
The Global Generative AI Awards celebrate and honor the most impactful and innovative applications of Generative AI across various sectors. Established to inspire excellence and progress in the field, the awards provide a global platform for recognizing visionary projects that push the boundaries of AI technology.
