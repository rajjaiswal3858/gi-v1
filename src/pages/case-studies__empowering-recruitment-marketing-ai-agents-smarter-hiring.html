Overview

An AI agent inside a programmatic advertising platform

Recruitics, an AI-powered recruitment marketing platform, partnered with HatchWorks AI to supercharge its programmatic job advertising capabilities by introducing an AI agent. With this new feature, Recruitics aimed to target the right candidates on the right platforms, automate job postings, optimize recruitment budgets, and ultimately elevate the client experience.

By integrating advanced algorithms, real-time data, and AI-driven automation, the partnership positioned Recruitics to differentiate its offering in a competitive market and deliver smarter, more efficient hiring outcomes.

The Challenge

Every posting had to reach qualified talent, efficiently

Recruitics needed a partner that combined robust software development expertise with advanced AI/ML engineering capabilities to evolve its platform. In short, it was seeking a strategic solution to serve its customers in new and improved ways, ensuring that every job posting reached qualified talent efficiently and effectively.

  1. 01

    Enhance platform capabilities

    Develop an AI agent that would not only automate job postings but also intelligently target the right candidates across various platforms.

  2. 02

    Improve operational efficiency

    Automate routine processes to optimize recruitment budgets and improve job ad distribution.

  3. 03

    Deliver a superior client experience

    Empower clients with faster response times and more personalized support through AI-driven automation.

The Process

A multi-phase approach: product development and AI innovation together

  1. 01

    Stakeholder engagement

    In-depth consultations to understand Recruitics' objectives and the requirements for an AI agent that enhances recruitment outcomes.

  2. 02

    Intelligent automation

    Leveraging real-time data, HatchWorks AI developed a framework to automate job postings and optimize budget allocation.

  3. 03

    AI agent development

    The team built a state-of-the-art AI agent using the LangChain framework for automated decision-making, enhanced with Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG) to pull relevant metadata from vector databases, semantic routers for dynamic workflow control, and a semantic cache to reduce latency and boost efficiency.

  4. 04

    Scalable cloud infrastructure

    The solution was deployed on Google Cloud Platform (GCP), complemented by real-time data visualization through Looker, with close communication maintained with stakeholders throughout.

The Outcome

Faster responses, personalized support, less overhead

The integration of the AI-powered agent has transformed the Recruitics platform, significantly enhancing the client experience and operational efficiency. Clients now enjoy faster response times, personalized support, and improved hiring outcomes.

The automation of routine tasks and the optimized distribution of job ads have led to substantial reductions in operational overhead. The solution is live for internal users and has been rolled out to Recruitics' customers, positioning the platform to deliver even greater value as adoption grows.
