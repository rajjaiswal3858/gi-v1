HatchWorks AI Named “Code Generative AI Solution of the Year” in the 2026 AI Breakthrough Awards

2026 AI Breakthrough Awards Winner

ATLANTA, June 25, 2026. HatchWorks AI has been named Code Generative AI Solution of the Year in the 2026 AI Breakthrough Awards, recognizing Generative-Driven Development, the firm's operating model for building production-grade software with AI.

Recognition for Code Generation That Reaches Production

The AI Breakthrough Awards are the longest-running recognition program dedicated to the artificial intelligence industry. Now in its ninth year and part of the Tech Breakthrough family of market intelligence programs, the 2026 edition drew more than 5,000 nominations from companies in over 20 countries. Entries are scored by an independent panel on innovation, performance, user experience, functionality, market impact, and value.

The Code Generative AI category recognizes solutions that apply generative AI to the creation of software itself. HatchWorks AI won for Generative-Driven Development, or GenDD, a methodology that defines how human engineers and AI agents divide the work across the full development lifecycle.

Most of the market is still measuring AI coding by how fast it produces lines. That is the wrong scoreboard. What our clients need is code that passes review, holds up in production, and shows a return they can point to. That is what GenDD was built to deliver, and it is what this award recognizes.

Brandon Powell, CEO and Founder of HatchWorks AI

What GenDD Does Differently

GenDD treats generative AI as part of the engineering system rather than a bolt-on assistant. In practice, that means:

  • Structured handoffs between engineers and AI agents at each stage of the lifecycle, from discovery through delivery.
  • Human review held at the points where risk concentrates, so speed does not come at the cost of governance.
  • Quality gates, testing, and documentation built into the loop instead of added after the fact.
  • Delivery metrics tracked sprint by sprint, giving engineering leaders and executives the same view of progress.
  • Deployment across nearshore teams in the Americas, working in real time with client engineering organizations.

Why This Matters for Enterprise Leaders

Enterprise teams have had access to AI coding tools for several years. The harder problem has been trust. Generated code that no one can review, maintain, or account for creates work rather than removing it, and that gap is where most AI development programs stall.

Recognition in this category points at that gap. It reflects a model built for organizations that need software to be fast and defensible at the same time, in environments where security, compliance, and reliability are not negotiable.

Our teams have been running this model across four countries and a range of industries. Every engagement sharpens it. Being recognized among the companies in this year's program tells us the approach is landing where it counts, which is inside real engineering organizations shipping real products.

Brandon Powell, CEO and Founder of HatchWorks AI

Built on AI-Native Software Development

Founded in 2016 and headquartered in Atlanta, HatchWorks AI operates across the Americas, combining product strategists, AI specialists, data engineers, and software developers into integrated delivery teams. The firm helps enterprises:

  • Develop AI-powered products, applications, and agents that embed intelligence into core workflows.
  • Deploy AI responsibly with an emphasis on transparency, security, bias mitigation, and ongoing monitoring.
  • Scale with nearshore engineering teams across the Americas for real-time collaboration and cost efficiency.
